## Week 1 goal: ONE conversation with a real CA

That's it. Not 10. Not 5. ONE.

If you get one CA on a phone/video call who listens to your pitch, you've succeeded in Week 1. Even if they don't buy. The conversation will teach you more than a month of planning.

---

## Week 2–4 goal: ONE paying customer

Get one CA to give you a real ASMT-10 notice → draft a reply using Claude.ai → send back → charge ₹999 via Razorpay.

When that ₹999 hits your bank account, you officially have a business.

---

## Month 2+ goal: Get to ₹50K MRR

Repeat the cycle. 5–10 customers × ₹999/notice × 5 notices each = ₹25K–₹50K MRR.

At this point, open `05-automation/` and start building the actual web app.

---

## The one rule

**Do not write a single line of code until you have your first paying customer.**

Every founder dies in the "I'll just polish the product before I sell" trap. You will too if you let yourself. The product is good enough RIGHT NOW with just Claude.ai and PDFs.

Sell first. Build second. Always.
