# Customer Intake Form

What to ask a CA when they say "yes, draft a reply for this notice." Send this as a WhatsApp message right after they share the notice PDF.

---

## The 6-question intake (copy-paste this message)

```
Great sir, draft start karta hoon. Bas 6 quick details chahiye:

1. Client business name (jo letter mein print hoga):

2. Client GSTIN:

3. Period in question (jaise: April 2025 to June 2025):

4. Notice deadline (DD/MM/YYYY):

5. Key facts mere liye — kya hua actually? (1-2 sentences mein, jaise
   "Vendor XYZ ne GSTR-1 late file kiya tha" ya "Genuine error tha
   accountant ka")

6. Defense ka tone? (Aggressive / Balanced / Conservative)

In 6 cheezo ka jawab dijiye, draft 24 ghante mein aapke pass hoga.
₹999 ka Razorpay link draft delivery ke saath share karunga.
```

---

## Why each question matters

**Q1 — Client business name:** Goes on the letterhead. Sometimes CAs use "M/s XYZ Pvt Ltd" or just "XYZ Industries" — match their convention.

**Q2 — GSTIN:** Critical for the reply header. Don't proceed without this. If they share notice PDF, you can OCR it yourself but always confirm.

**Q3 — Period in question:** Affects which CGST Rules version applies. GST has had many amendments — pre-2022 vs post-2022 rules differ on ITC.

**Q4 — Deadline:** You need this to:
- Prioritize your queue
- Set urgency expectations
- Charge premium if turnaround is < 24 hours (₹1,499 instead of ₹999)

**Q5 — Key facts:** This is the SECRET. AI alone can write a generic reply. AI + the human context of "what actually happened" produces a winning reply. Push for specifics here.

**Q6 — Tone:** Aggressive defenses ("we owe nothing, drop the case") work for clear-cut cases. Balanced ("most of the demand is incorrect, small portion we'll reverse") is the default. Conservative ("we accept the demand but request waiver of penalty") for cases where client made a real mistake.

---

## What to do if they don't give you enough info

Don't draft on incomplete info. Common gaps:

- **No deadline given:** Reply with "Sir, deadline kya hai? Critical hai for prioritization."
- **No GSTIN:** "GSTIN bhejein please, draft header ke liye chahiye."
- **No key facts:** "Sir, draft strong banane ke liye client ka side bhi chahiye — vendor late tha? Payment late tha? Document missing tha? 2-3 lines mein bata dijiye."

If they push back ("aap khud likh do, mein review karunga") — politely insist:
```
Sir, AI draft ki quality directly aapke input pe depend karti hai. Agar key
facts nahi hai, draft generic ho jayegi aur aapko bahut edit karna padega.
2 minute lagega aapko 5 questions ka answer dene mein, lekin draft 80% better
banegi. Please bata dijiye.
```

CAs respect founders who insist on quality over speed.

## Pricing tier signaling

After they answer Q1-Q6, check the notice complexity:

**Simple (₹999):**
- Single-issue notice
- Demand under ₹2 lakh
- Standard ITC mismatch / late filing

**Medium (₹1,999):**
- Multi-issue notice
- Demand ₹2-10 lakh
- Vendor coordination required
- Multiple periods affected

**Complex (₹4,999):**
- Demand above ₹10 lakh
- Multi-state operations
- Section 74 (fraud allegation) cases
- Multiple notice types combined

Send pricing message:
```
Sir, isko dekh ke lagta hai yeh [Simple / Medium / Complex] case hai.
Pricing ₹[999/1999/4999] hogi for this draft.

Aage badhe? Razorpay link share karta hoon — payment ke baad draft kal
shaam tak ready.
```

## Building trust with your first 5 customers

For first 5 customers, do these EXTRA things free:

1. Send the draft 12 hours early (overdeliver on time)
2. Include a "case strategy summary" — 3 bullet points on what arguments are strongest
3. Follow up 7 days later asking how the filing went
4. If they get a positive outcome, ask permission to use it (anonymously) as a testimonial
5. Send them a small "thank you for being first" — could be a ₹500 Amazon voucher when they pay #2

These cost you ₹500-1000 but turn early customers into evangelists. Word of mouth is your only marketing channel for the first 6 months.
