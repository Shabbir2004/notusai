Common questions

Is this software or service?

Today, it's a service powered by AI. You send us the notice, we use AI to do the heavy research and drafting, a human reviews every draft before delivery. As we scale, the same workflow will become a self-serve product.

Does the AI hallucinate case law?

AI is prompted to flag uncertain citations as "[verify before filing]" rather than fabricate. Every draft is human-reviewed for citation accuracy on Indian Kanoon before delivery.

Do you store my client's GST portal credentials?

No, never. We deliver a PDF draft. You upload to the GST portal yourself. We don't ask for or store any portal logins.

What if the draft quality is bad?

First draft is free for every new CA. If you find it unusable, no payment. Just give honest feedback so we can improve.

How is this different from Suvit / Vyapar TaxOne / ClearTax?

Those tools do bookkeeping automation and GST filing. They don't draft notice replies with legal-grade citations and case-law references. We focus specifically on the notice/litigation layer.

Do you serve large firms?

Currently working with small and mid-size CA firms. For larger firms with 10+ partners, please reach out for custom volume pricing.
