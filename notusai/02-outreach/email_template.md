# Email Outreach Template

Use when you have the CA's email (from their firm website, LinkedIn, CA directory).

---

## Subject lines to A/B test

Pick 3 and rotate. Track open rates in your sheet.

1. `Quick question about GST notice drafting at [Firm Name]`
2. `Free draft of any pending ASMT-10 — for [CA Name]`
3. `AI-drafted GST notice reply — sample for you`
4. `Saw your post on [topic] — quick build I'm working on`
5. `Cutting GST notice reply time from 4 hrs to 30 min — sample inside`

Best performers in my experience: #2 and #5.

---

```
you want to test it on a real notice, forward it directly to this email.

Best,
[Your Name]
+91 [Your Phone]
notusai.in
```

## Email tools (free)

- **Gmail** (free) — for sending
- **Streak CRM** (free Gmail plugin) — track opens, follow-up reminders
- **Hunter.io** (free for 25 searches/month) — find CA emails from firm websites
- **Mailtrack** (free) — see when emails are read

Avoid mass-mailing tools (Mailchimp, Lemlist) until you have 100+ leads — they cost money and your volume is too low to justify.

---

## How many emails per day?

- **Week 1:** 5/day (personalized, slow)
- **Week 2:** 10/day
- **Week 3:** 15/day + follow-ups
- **Week 4:** plateau at 15/day, focus on replies and demos

Don't send 50/day cold emails — your domain gets flagged, and personalization quality drops. 15 great emails > 100 mediocre ones.

---

## Where to get CA emails

1. **Firm websites** — most firms list "[name]@[firmname].co.in" — Hunter.io finds the format
2. **LinkedIn** — many CAs put their email in "Contact info"
3. **ICAI directory** — icai.org has searchable member directory (limited)
4. **CA conferences** — speakers/attendees often listed publicly
5. **CA YouTube channels** — many list business email in description

Avoid buying email lists. Quality is garbage, deliverability is terrible.
