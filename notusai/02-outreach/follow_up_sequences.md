# Follow-up Sequences

Most sales happen on follow-up #3-7. Don't quit after one message.

---

## The "rule of 7" for CA outreach

Each prospect should receive up to 7 touches across channels before you stop:

1. WhatsApp message
2. LinkedIn connection request
3. LinkedIn DM (after they accept)
4. Follow-up WhatsApp (Day 7)
5. Email (Day 14)
6. Voice call attempt (Day 21)
7. Final "soft close" message (Day 30)

Track in your sheet which touch each prospect is on.

---

## Sequence A — Warm lead (replied once, then went quiet)

These are gold. Don't lose them.

**Day 3 after their last reply:**
```
Sir, just checking in — koi pending notice hai jo aap forward kar sakte ho?
Test draft tayyar hai banana ke liye, just need a real notice to work on.
```

**Day 10:**
```
[Name] sir, ek thought share kar raha hoon — agar abhi koi notice nahi hai
aapke pas, no problem. Lekin agar aapke kisi colleague ya friend ke pas
pending hai, please mera number forward kar dena. Word of mouth se hi
shuruvat hoti hai.

Aap chahein to ek 5-min call kar lete hai — agar aapko interesting lage to
aapke firm mein bhi recommend kar sakte hai future mein.
```

**Day 21:**
```
[Name] sir, last check from my side.

Agar GST notice drafting abhi pain point nahi hai aapke practice mein,
totally fine — koi pressure nahi.

Bus phone number save kar lijiye. Jab kabhi deadline rush mein ho aur ek
ASMT-10 draft chahiye 4 ghante mein, bas WhatsApp ping kar dena.

Goodbye for now, take care.
```

## Sequence B — They asked for sample, never responded

Send the sample, then space out follow-ups.

**Day 3 after sending sample:**
```
Sir, sample dekha aapne? Koi feedback?
```

**Day 10:**
```
Sir, kya sample mein kuch missing tha aapke perspective se? Honest feedback
zyada valuable hai mere liye than the actual deal. 30 seconds bhi de sakte
ho to please reply karein.
```

**Day 20:**
```
[Name] sir, sample share kiya tha — agar pasand nahi aaya to please bata
dena, prompt improve karne ki zaroorat hai shayad. Aapki feedback se hi
product banta hai.
```

---

## Sequence C — They want demo, didn't book

**Day 2 (when they expressed interest):**
```
Sir, mere paas yeh slots free hai kal:
- 11 AM
- 2 PM  
- 4 PM

10 minute ki Google Meet call kar lete hai? Aap koi ek confirm kar dijiye.
```

**Day 5 (if still no time confirmed):**
```
Sir, jab bhi free ho 5-10 min, just whatsapp kar dijiye — main usi waqt
call kar lunga aapko. No need for advance scheduling.
```

**Day 12:**
```
[Name] sir, demo postpone hota ja raha hai — koi specific time work karega
aapke liye? Saturday/Sunday bhi chal jayega agar weekday tough hai.
```

## Sequence D — Cold lead, no response ever

**Day 0:** Initial WhatsApp
**Day 4:** LinkedIn connection
**Day 8:** LinkedIn DM (if connected)
**Day 14:** Email
**Day 21:** Final WhatsApp
```
[Name] sir, last message. Maine aapko reach out kiya tha few weeks back about
NotusAI — GST notice AI drafting tool. Agar relevant nahi hai aapke practice
ke liye, koi baat nahi.

Bus agar kabhi badle scenario mein zarurat lage, mera contact yaad rakhna.
Subah-shaam ki ek line share kar raha hoon: "Senior CAs ka asli kaam advisory
hai, junior ka kaam reconciliation. AI dono ko apne place pe rakhe."

Take care, sir.

— [Name]
notusai.in
```

This last message has a "thoughtful insight" quality. About 5-10% of cold leads respond to messages like this even after silence.

---

## Sequence E — They paid for first notice, no second notice yet

Within 7 days of first payment, follow up:

**Day 3:**
```
[Name] sir, kal ka draft kaise raha — koi heavy editing kiya aapne?
Honest feedback chahiye, prompt update karne ke liye.
```

**Day 10:**
```
Sir, kal aapne first notice diya tha, koi aur pending hai abhi?
Deadline ke pressure mein client ne kuch forward kiya ho to bhej dijiye.
```

**Day 20:**
```
Sir, monthly volume kitna chal raha hai notices ka? Agar 5-10 per month hai
to flat ₹4,999/month ka package bhi de sakta hoon — unlimited drafts.
Calculate kar lete hai jo aapke liye economical ho.
```

This last one is the SaaS conversion. After 1-2 ₹999 transactions, you can sell them a monthly plan.

## Tracking touches in your sheet

Add columns:
- Last touch date
- Last touch channel (WA/LI/Email/Call)
- Touch number (1-7)
- Status (Cold / Engaged / Demo Booked / Paid / Churned)
- Next action date
- Notes

Review your sheet every Monday morning. Send batch of follow-ups Tuesday morning. Most CAs check phones after morning client meetings, so 11 AM - 1 PM is a great window.

---

## When to stop

After 7 touches and 30 days, archive the lead. Don't waste mental energy chasing dead horses. Reactivate only when:
- They reach out to you
- A mutual connection vouches for you
- They post something on LinkedIn about a pain you solve (perfect reply opportunity)

Founder time is your most valuable asset. Spend it on leads showing signal.
