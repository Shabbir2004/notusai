# LinkedIn Outreach

LinkedIn is your highest-quality channel. CAs have profiles, you can see their seniority, their firm, and you can connect first.

---

## How to find your targets

Search filters on LinkedIn:
- Title: "Chartered Accountant" OR "Partner" OR "Tax Consultant"
- Location: [Your city + nearby cities]
- Industry: Accounting / Financial Services / Legal Services
- Connections: 2nd or 3rd degree (warmer than 4th)

Build a list of 50 targets in a Google Sheet (use template in `06-tracking/`).

---

## The connection request (when sending invite)

Keep it SHORT (LinkedIn limits to 300 chars on free tier).

```
Hi [Name], working on AI tool for Indian CAs to auto-draft GST notice replies
with section + case law citations. Would love to learn from your practice
experience. Connecting :)
```

Accept rate: 30-50% if your profile looks legit. Spend 2 hours making your LinkedIn profile look professional first.

---

## First DM (after they accept)

Wait 1-2 days after they connect. Don't pitch immediately.

```
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!

Quick context — I'm building NotusAI, a tool that drafts replies to GST notices
(ASMT-10, DRC-01, Section 73 SCNs) using AI with proper CGST Act + case law
citations. Currently working with a few early CA firms.

Curious — does your firm see a lot of GST scrutiny notices? Would love your
honest take on whether something like this would be useful or if existing
tools already solve this for you.

Happy to share a sample draft if you're curious.

— [Your Name]
```

---

## Second DM (if they reply positively)

```
Awesome, thanks [Name].

Here's how it works in 60 seconds:

1. You forward me an ASMT-10/DRC-01 notice (any pending one in your pipeline)
2. I draft a formal reply within 24 hours — with CGST sections, CBIC circulars,
   and 2-3 relevant HC/CESTAT case laws
3. You review and decide — if quality is good, ₹999/notice from next time.
   If not, no commitment.

First one is on me. Just to prove the concept.

Want to send me one to try? Or shall we hop on a 10-min call first to show
you a sample draft?
```

---

## Follow-up sequence (if no reply)

**Day 7 (if first DM ignored):**
```
Hi [Name], just bumping this up in case it got buried.

If GST notice drafting isn't a pain point right now no worries — happy to
just stay connected. But if you do have a tough one in your queue, drafting
one free is genuinely how we're getting early feedback.

Either way — have a good week.
```

**Day 21 (final follow-up):**
```
[Name], last note from my side.

In case it's relevant later — sharing a sample I drafted for a hypothetical
ASMT-10 last week: [attach PDF]

You'll see the level of section citation + case law reference. If at any point
you want to test it on a real one, drop me a message. No hurry.

— [Your Name]
```

After that, stop. Don't be a pest. Some will respond in 6 months when they hit a painful notice. Plant seeds.

## Optimizing for your LinkedIn profile

Before you DM anyone, make sure your profile says:

**Headline:** *"Building NotusAI — AI-drafted GST notice replies for Indian CA firms"*

**About section:**
```
Fresh graduate building tools at the intersection of AI and Indian tax practice.

NotusAI helps CA firms draft formal replies to GST notices (ASMT-10, DRC-01,
Section 73/74 SCNs) in 24 hours instead of 4-8 hours of manual drafting.
Every reply includes proper CGST Act section citations, CBIC circulars, and
relevant HC/CESTAT case laws.

Currently working with [N] CA firms across [cities]. Open to early design
partner conversations.

Drop me a message or notusai.in for samples.
```

When you have ZERO customers, write: "Currently in early conversations with 5+ firms — looking for design partners."

When you have 1 customer: "Working with 1 firm in Pune, expanding."

When you have 5: "Working with 5+ firms across Pune, Mumbai, Bangalore."

Never lie. Always understate. Reality compounds faster than you think.

---

## How to make your LinkedIn profile not look like a fresh-grad

1. **Photo:** professional, business attire, neutral background, smiling
2. **Banner:** simple — your product logo or "NotusAI — AI notice drafting for CAs" in plain text
3. **Headline:** as above
4. **Education:** keep it brief
5. **Experience:** add "Founder, NotusAI — May 2026 - Present"
6. **Posts:** start posting 2x/week about:
   - GST case law summaries you find interesting
   - "I drafted 50 ASMT-10 replies this month — here are 3 patterns I see"
   - Behind-the-scenes building NotusAI
   - Comparisons of AI-drafted vs manual replies

This content is FREE marketing. Every CA who sees you commenting intelligently on GST is a potential lead.
