# WhatsApp Outreach Scripts

These are your sales scripts. Personalize the [bracketed] parts.

---

## Cold message #1 — to a CA you DON'T know personally

Use after finding their number on LinkedIn / Google Maps / CA directory.

```
Namaste [CA Surname] sir,

Main [Your Name] hoon, [Your City] se. Apologies for the cold message.

Main ek tool bana raha hoon jo GST notices (ASMT-10, DRC-01, scrutiny) ka
reply AI se draft karta hai — with proper section citations, CGST rules,
aur relevant HC/CESTAT case laws.

Aapke firm mein har month kitne GST notices aate hai approximately?

Agar koi ek pending notice hai jiska reply abhi draft karna hai, main free mein
draft karke de sakta hoon. Sirf 24 ghante mein. Agar aapko quality acchi lage,
phir ₹999 per notice charge karunga from next time onwards. Otherwise no commitment.

Interested? 2 minute ki call kar lete hai?

— [Your Name]
[Your Phone]
[Your Email]
```

## Cold message #3 — when you have a warm intro

When uncle/family friend/college contact gives you the CA's number.

```

[Mutual contact name] ne aapka number diya. Maine unko bataya ki main ek tool
bana raha hoon jo GST notices ka reply AI se draft karta hai (with proper

work mein involved rehte hai.

Kal kab free hai sir?

— [Your Name]
```

## Response handling — what to say when they reply

### If they reply "what is this?"
```
Sir, simple use case: aap mujhe ek scrutiny notice ya ASMT-10 forward karte hai
WhatsApp pe (PDF ya photo). Main client details + key facts puchunga aapse
(5 questions). Phir 24 ghante mein aapko ek complete formal reply draft milta
hai PDF mein — with proper CGST Act sections, CBIC circulars, HC/CESTAT case
laws cited. Aap review karke client ke letterhead pe convert karke file kar
sakte hai.

Pricing ₹999 per notice (Simple), ₹1,999 (Medium - multi-issue), ₹4,999 (Complex).
First one free for quality check. Sound interesting?
```

### If they reply "how does AI do this — koi human nahi?"
```
Sir, AI does the heavy lifting (research, structure, citations), main personally
review karta hoon every draft before sending. Think of it as: aapko ek junior
CA mil raha hai jo 4 ghante ka kaam 30 min mein karta hai, aur main quality check
karta hoon.

Final review aur signature obviously aapki hi rahegi — main sirf draft deta hoon.
Aapke professional judgment ka koi substitute nahi hai.
```

### If they reply "send sample"
```
Sir, ek sample share kar raha hoon — yeh hypothetical ASMT-10 ke liye draft hai
[attach a sample PDF you generated earlier with the master prompt + sample_asmt10.md].

Aapke pass agar ek real pending notice hai, mujhe forward karein — main aapke
actual case ke liye draft karke 24 ghante mein bhejta hoon. First one
completely free.
```

### If they reply "kitne logo ke saath kaam kar rahe ho?"
**Be honest. Don't fake it.**
```
Sir, abhi shuruvat kar raha hoon — aap pehle 5 CAs mein se ek banoge agar
aap try karein. Iss liye first one free hai aur pricing aggressive hai.
Goal hai ki next 3 months mein 20-30 CAs ke saath kaam karke product
improve karna. Aapki honest feedback mere liye bahut valuable hogi.
```

This kind of honesty actually wins more deals than fake credibility. CAs respect builders.

### If they reply "abhi busy hoon, baad mein contact karunga"
```
Bilkul sir, koi pressure nahi. Aapke pass jab koi pending ASMT-10/DRC-01 ho,
just ping me — 24 hour mein free draft mil jayega.

Aapka number save kar raha hoon. Next month deadline rush ke time agar yaad
aaye to message kar dijiye.
```

### If they say "demo dikhao"
```
Sir, 5 minute ka video call (Google Meet) ya WhatsApp video — kis pe convenient
hai aapko? Kab free hai aaj/kal? Just 2-3 timeslots batayein, main book kar leta hoon.
```

## When NOT to push

If a CA:
- Says "main software use nahi karta" → leave them, move on
- Doesn't reply after 2 messages → leave 7 days, then ONE more message, then stop
- Asks for too much free work → politely decline ("first one free, but I have to charge from 2nd onwards")

Your time is finite. Better to find 5 enthusiastic CAs than convince 50 reluctant ones.

---

## Daily target

- **Week 1:** 5 messages/day, 35 total
- **Week 2:** 10 messages/day, 70 total
- **Week 3:** 10/day + follow-ups to non-responders
- **Week 4:** Hopefully 2-3 calls booked, 1 paying customer

If after 4 weeks of consistent outreach you have ZERO responses, the issue is the audience (you're messaging wrong CAs) or the pitch. Come back and refine.
